Projects / Live Demo

Explore the Police Medical Unit – Patient Information Management System (PIMS), a web-based system designed to organize police patient records, support medical certificate processing, and help authorized personnel manage patient information. Each role-based demo below highlights the system's main functions.

Police Patient

View recent health information, laboratory results, and check-up history; update basic profile details; and submit medical certificate requests and check their status. Validated medical certificates can be verified using their QR code.

Police Patient demo video

Police Nurse

Register police patient accounts and maintain patient records by recording vital signs, laboratory test results, and check-up information. The nurse can also review and process medical certificate requests as part of the certificate workflow.

Police Nurse demo video

Regional Doctor

Review patient evaluations, record diagnoses and treatment recommendations, manage check-up results, create announcements, validate medical certificates, and generate QR codes for approved certificates. The doctor can also review patient statistics.

Regional Doctor demo video

Admin

Manage authorized Medical Unit accounts, maintain police patient records, archive inactive patients or personnel transferred outside the region, and view system statistics such as registered personnel, police patients, and patient distribution by location.

Admin demo video
